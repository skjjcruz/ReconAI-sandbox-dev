DHQ Value (0-10,000) is a dynasty trade value computed from YOUR league's actual data.

How it's calculated:
• 40% Production — weighted PPG from the last 5 seasons
• 25% Age curve — position-specific peak windows
• 20% Situation — roster status, role, trajectory
• 10% Scarcity — supply vs demand at position
• 5% Peak bonus — years of prime remaining
